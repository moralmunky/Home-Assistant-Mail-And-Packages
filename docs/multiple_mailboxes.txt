SEVERAL MAILBOXES
Mail and Packages setup notes

Add each mailbox as its own entry. Each one is named after its address, for
example sensor.someone_example_com_mail_usps_packages, and gets its own image
folder automatically.

Once two or more mailboxes are set up, an "All mailboxes" entry shows up under
Settings, Devices & services, Discovered. Adding it creates combined sensors,
for example sensor.all_mailboxes_mail_usps_packages.

How the combined numbers work:

    A package that shows up in more than one mailbox is counted once. It is
    matched by tracking number, or by order number for Amazon. This covers
    shipping emails that get forwarded between your accounts.

    Counts that have no tracking number, such as the USPS daily mail count,
    are added up across mailboxes.

    Each combined sensor lists every mailbox's own count in a "mailboxes"
    attribute, and the tracking numbers it found in "tracking_#".

The summary does not read any email itself. It recalculates whenever one of
your mailboxes finishes a scan, so it adds no load on your mail servers. When
you add a carrier to any mailbox, the summary picks it up on its own.

If you click Ignore on the Discovered card, you can still add it later from
the ignored integrations. The summary has no settings of its own; removing it
does not touch your mailboxes.
